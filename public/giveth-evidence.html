What Is CPS-0001?

CPS-0001 defines the Continuity Receipt — a cryptographically verifiable statement that an observer collected sufficient evidence supporting the continuity of a subject over a bounded interval of time. It answers a question existing identity infrastructure cannot: "Is this subject the same entity observed 8 seconds ago?"

The protocol is engine-independent — any sensor, algorithm, or hardware can produce a valid receipt. If one evidence engine is compromised, others can be substituted without changing the receipt format.

Technical Architecture

Producer (any engine) → Continuity Receipt → Verifier (V₁-V₇) → Consumer Decision

Verification Contract:
  V₁: Signature validity (Ed25519)
  V₂: Receipt integrity (SHA-256)
  V₃: Temporal validity (not expired)
  V₄: Chain continuity (predecessor hash)
  V₅: Minimum confidence threshold
  V₆: Engine eligibility
  V₇: Subject consistency
