> **Researching continuity as a verifiable property of the digital world.**

The Continuity Lab is a research organization. We are not a company. No product. No funding.

---

## What We Do

We study whether **continuity** — the unbroken chain of physical presence — can be made a verifiable property of digital existence.

Our first publication is **CPS-0001 (Continuity Protocol Standard)**, an engine-independent protocol object for continuity assertions.
